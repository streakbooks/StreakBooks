# Gym capability comparison

This is an original comparison of capability areas. It does not copy product names into the interface and it does not copy another product's screens.

| Area | Typical fitness platforms | Typical accounting platforms | StreakBooks Gym |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Member register | Strong | Weak | Members link to contacts. A child requires a guardian. The payer can differ from the member. |
| Plans and contracts | Strong | Weak | New prices create a version. Existing contracts keep their price. |
| Scheduling | Strong | Rare | Trainer and room overlaps are rejected in the database. Full classes waitlist. |
| Recurring billing | Strong, often via their own processor | Strong for generic invoices | Membership invoices and receipts post to the ledger. Automatic card charging is not connected. |
| Revenue recognition | Often cash-based | Deferred revenue in larger products | Prepaid memberships defer, then recognise on a schedule. Cancellation does not earn the balance unless the contract forfeits it. |
| Member statements | Common | Common for customers | Opening, charges, receipts and closing come from the gym subledger and are checked against the mapped receivable account. |
| Payroll and inventory | Often separate products | Native | Trainer pay posts a payable. Payroll, stock and depreciation stay in those modules. |
| Multi-entity | Franchise tools vary | Consolidation in larger products | A branch can be marked franchise. A different legal entity stays a different organisation. |

Gaps that remain open: verified card acquiring, door hardware, stock movements, statutory payroll, and public checkout.
