# Settings coverage matrix

**Updated:** 2026-11-27
**Evidence rule:** a capability is `IMPLEMENTED` only when the code registry names a UI, persistence mechanism, server validation, permission, audit record, a real consumer and a behavior test. Otherwise it remains `PARTIAL`, `PLACEHOLDER`, `MISSING`, `BLOCKED` or `NOT APPLICABLE`. Category badges do not promote individual settings.

## Capabilities with complete evidence

The machine-readable subset is `src/lib/settings/capability-registry.ts`; its contract tests are in `src/lib/settings/capability-registry.test.ts`.

Organisation identity retains partial overall coverage. The database blocks base-currency changes after posted journals and serializes that setting write against the authoritative journal-posting currency read. Legal name has a tested document-branding consumer; fiscal year end drives accounting report/period dates; locale and date format have audited writes and customer-document consumers. IANA time zones are now database-validated on every company write and have tested company-local date consumers. Country and tax-regime/filing/reporting coverage remain partial; see `tests/integration/settings-company-identity-atomic.sql`.

## Known gaps

The machine-readable `SETTINGS_GAPS` entries track: remaining country and legacy tax-summary consumer evidence; unenforced accounting duplicate/control warnings and auto-credit settings (manual-journal memo, backdate and direct bank/cash warnings are implemented); tax regime/source-of-truth, jurisdiction, and jurisdiction-specific filing/reporting consumers (tax rate/code master history and rate selection are implemented); incomplete sales, purchase and expense typed settings; payroll schedule/component ownership and broader module coverage beyond its core configuration; central banking settings not proven to drive every module control; MFA/idle timeout preferences without end-to-end auth enforcement and support-access consent without a client-controlled approval workflow; provider connection delivery evidence; and account-wide preference scope. Purchase-order three-way matching now has a fail-closed authorization consumer. Colour mode and validated custom/named palettes sync per user and organisation from the profile.

Further gaps recorded in `docs/settings/audit.md` include inventory valuation source-of-truth, tax/regional limitations, fixed-asset and time workflows, approval coverage, exports/retention, module-level settings ownership, full screen-reader and mobile verification, and legacy route compatibility. Promote a row only with a behavior-changing test and evidence for all fields in the capability contract.

## Typed field inventory

`SETTING_FIELD_COVERAGE` is generated from every key in `CATEGORY_SCHEMAS` and checked against the schema shapes by `validateSettingsCapabilityRegistry()`. Each record includes the category, route, schema, persistence path, audit record and consumer evidence status. A field is editable only if it also appears in the complete capability registry. Other typed fields are shown as **Partial** and disabled. This is a deliberate safety state, not a claim that those features are complete.

| Typed field status | Count | Meaning                                                                                   |
| ------------------ | ----: | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Complete           |    12 | Accounting backdate and direct bank/cash warnings, reporting basis and code padding; Purchases supplier summary and three-way matching; Reports comparison; Notifications sender name, reply-to and quiet-hours fields have tested consumers. Payroll configuration is a separate complete capability, not part of the typed category schemas. |
| Partial            |    32 | Persisted schema keys without the full field-level access and consumer proof.             |

The separate complete capabilities `accounting.lock_policy` and `features.classes` are not fields in `CATEGORY_SCHEMAS`; they use their own established settings panels and guarded persistence paths.
